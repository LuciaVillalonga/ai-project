How can AI-generated work be spotted?

Unfortunately, there is no concrete solution to spotting AI-generated texts or images, even AI detecting tools are inaccurate most of the time! With how accurate it has gotten today, even images or videos made with AI can appear to be very realistic. Some ways to try to detect its usage is to recognize the structure used, potential fake information, or image context that does not look or sound realistic. However, this does not verify that the information is AI or human made. This has caused a lot of problems, especially in areas of politics and governance, with the use of AI creating misleading content that can fool the public.
