Can AI be creative?

AI’s ability to produce artistic work and recreate works from famous artists is impressive, but could it be considered creative? The work of a human has something that AI cannot replace: authenticity. The attention to detail and originality that humans have will remain to be uncopiable by a machine. This is not to say that AI cannot generate fine works, according to The Washington Post. When combining a human prompt with the millions of images available to the software online, AI can perfectly generate a very realistic image. Since works are created based on the patterns it learns and the images available on the internet, it cannot function without the prompt or creative input of a human being.
