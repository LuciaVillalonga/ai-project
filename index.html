What is artificial intelligence?

Artificial intelligence is a technologically advanced computer software that performs tasks that are human-like such as problem solving, creating new ideas, organizing information, creativity, and decision making. AI is constantly being updated with new information so that it can respond to human language, identify objects and pick up on patterns, and perform other tasks that could potentially replace human intelligence. It´s significance has grown so much over the last years that it is now integrated in our daily lives through social media, the internet, work, music, art, and much more.
