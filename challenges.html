What are its benefits? What are its challenges and risks?

However, it still faces many challenges, the biggest one being that it lacks complete accuracy and therefore is not fully trusted nor reliable. Since it is trained by humans, certain information makes the software biassed, especially in the areas of politics, healthcare, and education. It also has privacy risks as it stores a lot of information from the user and picks up on information from the algorithm, which can then create deepfakes. The use of AI is also hard to detect which can impact education and jobs. Lastly, its models require a lot of power usage which can impact the environment negatively with its excessive water and energy consumption. An article on the New York Times claims, ‘tech companies are investing billions of dollars to build energy-hungry data centers at a time where the demand for electricity in the United States is already increasing,’ its investment in growth and rise to power is clearly prioritized over society and environmental needs.
